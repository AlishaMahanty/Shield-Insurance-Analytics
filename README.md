# Shield Insurance Pilot Project Analytics

## Project Overview

This project features an **interactive Power BI dashboard** for Shield Insurance Analytics, showcasing **customer trends, revenue, sales channels, and age group insights**. 

Completed during my Virtual Internship at Codebasics, the dashboard delivers **actionable insights**, clear **analytical storytelling**, and **interactive analytics** for management and business users.

## Business Objectives

- Track and analyze **customer growth** and **revenue trends** over time.  
- Evaluate the performance of **different sales channels** and customer segments.  
- Understand **age group behavior** and settlement patterns to inform policy strategies.  
- Provide **interactive visual insights** that support data-driven decisions for stakeholders.

## Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is organized into **three main pages**, accessible via the **Home Page** navigation:

1. **General Overview** – High-level KPIs including total customers, total revenue, daily growth rates, and monthly trends. City-wise and age group-wise breakdowns provide actionable insights at a glance.  

2. **Sales Mode Analysis** – Visualizes customer and revenue distribution across sales channels, with monthly trends for each mode to assess channel performance.  

3. **Age Group Analysis** – Detailed view of age-wise customer trends, settlement percentages, policy preferences, and sales mode comparisons for each age group.
